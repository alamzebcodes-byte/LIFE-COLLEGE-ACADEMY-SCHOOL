About Us

Why Choose Life College?

Your trusted partner in academic and professional excellence

Shaping Futures, Building Dreams

Life College Academic & School is a premier educational institute committed to delivering excellence in both traditional academics and modern skill-based training. Located at Javed Bahria Main Gate, our institute stands as a beacon of quality education for students of all ages.

We offer a complete educational journey — from School level to Intermediate in Pre Engineering, Pre Medical, Arts, and Commerce. Alongside academics, we provide industry-oriented professional courses including MS Office, Typing, Web Development with AI, Content Creation, SEO Searching, and English Language to prepare our students for the challenges of the modern world.
